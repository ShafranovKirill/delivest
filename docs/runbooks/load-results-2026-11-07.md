Process RSS changed 179,996→182,208, 318,256→221,276, and 433,416→297,632 KiB. These short windows do not establish memory or backlog stability.

This is a safe subset and a short exploratory result, not a confirmed capacity limit. The measured cart/order path selected plain products; the menu fixture includes modifier choices, but modifier checkout was not exercised. Account, delivery, bonuses, online payment, sockets, provider delays/faults, cold-cache spike, N04 fault recovery, lock-wait/pool-saturation metrics, and a pilot were not tested. Full N02 still requires 5 min warmup, 30 min stable stages, 5 min spike, ≥2 h soak, and three comparable repetitions. Same-host generator, dev runtime, synthetic proxy IPs, and 2-minute duration prevent a production capacity promise.
